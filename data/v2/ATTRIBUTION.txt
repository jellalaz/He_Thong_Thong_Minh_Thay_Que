Two public research datasets used by the V2 experiment (downloaded 13 November 2026):

Pima Indians Diabetes Database: OpenML dataset 37, version 1.
https://www.openml.org/d/37
Original source: NIDDK; historical donor Vincent Sigillito / Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory.
OpenML metadata labels the licence "Public"; this project does not relabel it as a different licence.
The included pima_metadata.json preserves the source attribution and original study description.
768 records, 8 input features, tested_negative / tested_positive.
Restricted historical population: females at least 21 years old of Pima heritage.

Early Stage Diabetes Risk Prediction: UCI dataset 529, DOI 10.24432/C5VG8H.
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/529/early+stage+diabetes+risk+prediction+dataset
M. M. F. Islam, Rahatara Ferdousi, Sadikur Rahman, Humayra Yasmin Bushra.
UCI lists CC BY 4.0. 520 questionnaire records from Sylhet Diabetes Hospital, Bangladesh.
The V2 experiment removes repeated feature rows before splitting, leaving 251 rows.

sources/ contains the downloaded files and SHA-256 metadata.
pima.csv and early_stage.csv contain the cleaned numeric inputs, target and fixed split.
No data was generated to replace missing real source data. No patient identifier is included.
These sources are not representative Vietnamese clinical validation datasets.
